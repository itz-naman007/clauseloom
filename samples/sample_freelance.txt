FICTIONAL SAMPLE — WEB DEVELOPMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT
This fictional agreement is between client Example Studio and freelancer Kiran Sample for a small business website. It is for demonstration only.

1. SCOPE AND FEE
The freelancer will deliver a five-page website. The project fee is INR 80,000. The project duration is 8 weeks.

2. PAYMENT
Invoices are payable net-90 after submission. Payment is subject to the client's satisfaction at the client's sole discretion.

3. REVISIONS
The client may request unlimited revisions and unlimited changes until the client considers the website finished.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property rights transfer to the client upon creation, before payment. The freelancer assigns all pre-existing tools and background materials used in the project to the client.

5. RESTRICTIONS
A non-compete applies for 24 months throughout India after termination. The freelancer must not solicit any customer of the client during that period.

6. TERMINATION
The client may terminate immediately without notice. The freelancer must provide 60 days written notice and complete all requested work before leaving.

7. LIABILITY
The freelancer accepts unlimited liability and indemnifies the client against all claims and losses connected with the website. Liquidated damages apply to any breach.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY
Confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely with no end date, including ordinary business information.

9. DISPUTES
Arbitration in London will resolve all disputes. The freelancer bears all arbitration costs regardless of outcome.

10. RENEWAL AND EXCLUSIVITY
The services automatically renew each year. Exclusivity prevents the freelancer from working with any other client during the engagement.
