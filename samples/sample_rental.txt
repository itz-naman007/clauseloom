FICTIONAL SAMPLE — RESIDENTIAL LEAVE-AND-LICENSE AGREEMENT
This fictional agreement is between landlord Mira Example and tenant Dev Sample for a residential flat in Pune, Maharashtra. It is for demonstration only.

1. TERM AND RENT
The agreement has a term of 11 months commencing 1 January 2027. Monthly rent is INR 20,000, payable on the fifth day of each month.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The security deposit is INR 60,000. The deposit is non-refundable. The landlord may deduct charges at sole discretion without itemized receipts.

3. LOCK-IN
The lock-in period is 12 months for the tenant only. The tenant must pay the remaining rent if leaving early, even though the stated agreement term is shorter.

4. NOTICE
The tenant must give 90 days written notice. The landlord must give 15 days written notice.

5. RENEWAL
This agreement will automatically renew for another term. Rent will increase by 15% annually upon renewal.

6. ACCESS
The landlord may enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspections.

7. EARLY EXIT
Early exit results in forfeiture of the deposit and a penalty equal to two months of rent.

8. REPAIRS
The tenant is responsible for all repairs including structural repairs and replacement of major plumbing systems, regardless of cause.

9. LATE CHARGES
A late fee of 3% per month applies to unpaid rent. Interest is compounded monthly.

10. HOUSE RULES
No pets, overnight guests or subletting are permitted. Both parties acknowledge these rules before signing.
